## Company release decisions

Before a live launch, ask the company to:

- Confirm estate names, spelling, locations, currently offered plot sizes and stock.
- Confirm feature delivery, current site/development status and approved recent photos.
- Supply current outright and instalment terms with a complete inclusions/charges statement and checked date.
- Approve exact design-to-plot associations and resolve source bedroom contradictions.
- Supply approved brochures, floor plans, map coordinates and documentation wording where wanted.
- Approve image reuse, testimonials and any news before publication.
- Review and approve privacy/terms text and define data retention and enquiry handling.
- Configure live mail delivery and verify that staff receive notifications.

No launch, emails, WhatsApp messages, or contact with company staff was performed as part of this build.
