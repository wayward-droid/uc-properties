# Staff guide

1. **Locations** — maintain the location names used by filters and estate records.
2. **Estates** — add name, stable URL name, location, summary, full description, availability and current development status. Add the cover and classify it accurately as rendering or photograph. Publish only reviewed content.
3. **Plots & pricing** — create the estate's exact plot/property options. Mark an offer as a completed home only when that is what is being sold. Bedroom filters apply to completed homes.
4. **Pricing** — leave unconfirmed amounts blank. Verified pricing requires outright price, checked date, inclusions, and extra-charge statement. An instalment plan also needs total, deposit, duration and schedule. The site does not calculate invented payment plans or take payments.
5. **Building designs** — add approved design text, bedroom counts, specifications, rendering and optional floor plan. Clearly explain land vs construction vs completed-home inclusions.
6. **Design compatibility** — associate a specific plot option with a specific design. Record the approval basis in private notes, then check the approval box. An unapproved record is not offered publicly as a compatible design.
7. **Images & documents** — attach each file to exactly one estate or design. Give images meaningful alternative text. Use rendering, photograph, floor plan or brochure classification correctly. Brochures must be PDFs.
8. **Enquiries & inspections** — open a reference, review the details, contact the visitor outside the dashboard, then update New / Contacted / Scheduled / Completed / Cancelled. Add staff-only notes. Changing a status does not send a customer message. Confirm inspections directly with the visitor before marking them Scheduled.
9. **FAQs** — use `{{phone}}`, `{{email}}` and `{{address}}` when mentioning shared contact details, so Company settings update the answer too.
10. **News & updates** — write the actual update and publish it. Text is stored and rendered as plain text; arbitrary HTML is escaped.
11. **Testimonials** — both authenticity/permission approval and publication must be selected. There are no invented customer quotes in the initial data.
12. **Company settings** — maintain phone, display format, WhatsApp, email, office address and introduction. Supply the approved privacy and terms text, then check legal-copy approval to remove the draft notices.

Deleting an estate removes its options, media records and compatibility links. The confirmation page requires typing DELETE. Customer enquiries are retained; links to removed records become empty. Uploaded files are retained for manual review to avoid deleting shared or still-needed files accidentally.

## Updated website behaviour

Inspection buttons now open WhatsApp directly with the selected published estate/plot/design. A direct link does not create a dashboard record. Visitors can still use the contact and inspection forms: successful submissions save a record as before, then open WhatsApp with the submitted details and reference. A dashboard record does **not** prove the visitor pressed Send in WhatsApp. Appointment confirmation remains a staff action.

The interactive map uses your estate latitude and longitude when both are provided and valid. Without supplied coordinates, the three existing locations use clearly labelled approximate area centres. They are not survey coordinates or exact estate entrances. Add precise, company-approved coordinates in the estate editor to show the actual estate location. New locations without coordinates remain in the estate list with directions on request.
